## About

We study how intelligent systems perceive, represent, and reason about the visual world. Our research brings together 3D and 4D perception, efficient adaptation of foundation models, multimodal reasoning, and brain-inspired learning. We aim to build models that learn from limited supervision, transfer across tasks, and use computation efficiently.

## Research

- **3D and 4D perception:** Learning geometry and motion from point clouds for registration, scene understanding, action recognition, and spatiotemporal segmentation.
- **Self-supervised representation learning:** Developing masked modeling, diffusion-based pretraining, and multimodal supervision for static and dynamic point clouds.
- **Efficient foundation models:** Studying parameter-efficient transfer learning, memory-efficient adaptation, and architectures for object- and scene-level understanding.
- **Multimodal reasoning:** Connecting visual evidence with language reasoning and compressing reasoning traces while preserving task-relevant information.
- **Brain-inspired intelligence:** Exploring spiking neural networks and their role in efficient visual information processing.
